# 04 — BM25 Comparison

**Original report:** `/Users/atharvapatil/ledger/RAG-bm25/reports/retrieval/bm25/bm25_report.md` + `depth_report.md` (worktree `experiment/bm25`, commit e633315). Summary also in `reports/comparison/summary.md` § overall/multi.

## 1. Objective
Evaluate BM25 lexical retrieval under identical privacy preprocessing, dataset, and v2 ground truth as Dense, apples-to-apples.

## 2. Dataset / Query Population
- Same 120 MRN records, 605 queries (601 single + 4 true multi + 1 single-target aggregate). Same `ground_truth_v2.json`.

## 3. Method
- **BM25Okapi** (rank-bm25, default k1=1.5 b=0.75) over tokenized documents (`re.findall(r"\w+", text.lower())` after masking).
- **Configs:** `bm25_raw` (no masking) vs `bm25_masked` (medical policy `mask_text` before chunk/tokenize). 1 record = 1 document = 1 chunk (120 docs). Deterministic tie-break `(-score, record_id, chunk_index)`. K=1,3,5,10,20,30,50.

## 4. Experimental Controls
- Same corpus/queries/ground-truth/metrics as Dense; only retrieval algorithm changed. No hybrid.

## 5. Metrics
- HitRate, Precision, Recall, MRR (record-level deduplicated), depth per-aggregate, coverage thresholds.

## 6. Main Numerical Results

Overall (605, bm25_raw; bm25_masked identical):
- k=1: HitRate 0.0132 Prec 0.0132 Rec 0.0087 MRR 0.0132
- k=3: 0.0331 /0.0149 /0.0279 /0.0218
- k=5: 0.0496 /0.0149 /0.0455 /0.0255
- k=10: 0.0909 /0.0144 /0.0887 /0.0308
- k=20: 0.1736 /0.0133 /0.1736 /0.0363
- k=30: 0.2562 /0.0116 /0.2562 /0.0396
- k=50: 0.4215 /0.0103 /0.4215 /0.0438

Per-aggregate (multi-record mean 5 aggregates):
- k=2→0.31, k=5→0.50, k=10→0.73, k=20→1.00 (both configs identical).

Masking raw vs masked: **identical** at all K (0 difference); medical masking preserves discriminative terms.

Coverage k for 80/90/100 (both configs):
- AMLODIPINE 17: 20/20/20, METFORMIN 7: 10/10/10, PARACETAMOL 20: 20/20/20, HYPERTENSION 16: 20/20/20, T2D+HYP 1: 2/2/2.

Comparison to Dense at k=10 (from summary): Dense 0.0888 recall /0.0325 MRR, BM25 0.0887/0.0308 → virtually identical; at k=50 both 0.4215.

## 7. Interpretation
BM25 is competitive but does not provide meaningful overall improvement over Dense on this dataset; lexical discriminative terms (drug/diagnosis) make aggregates easy for both; single-record poor due to templated generic queries.

## 8. Limitations
- Default BM25 params not tuned; simple word regex tokenizer; 1-chunk/record assumption.
